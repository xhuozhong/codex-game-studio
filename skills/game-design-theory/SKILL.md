# Game Design & Theory

Own the question: "Why will the player want to do this again?"

## Core loop

For every proposed feature define:
- player intention
- action
- immediate feedback
- short-term reward
- meaningful consequence
- reason to repeat or branch

## Progression

Prefer visible, understandable progression. Avoid adding currencies, stats or crafting layers unless they create a meaningful decision.

## Choice design

Choices should trade something off: time, risk, resources, relationships, information or opportunity. Cosmetic choices are fine when the experience calls for them, but do not pretend they are systemic choices.

## Difficulty

Increase complexity through new decisions and interactions before relying on arbitrary stat inflation.

## Review questions

- Is the player's next action obvious?
- Is the reward proportional to effort?
- Can the player understand cause and effect?
- Is there meaningful agency?
- Does the feature support the game's fantasy?
- Does it create a reason to return?

## Vertical slice rule

Prototype the smallest version that can prove the loop before building content around it.
